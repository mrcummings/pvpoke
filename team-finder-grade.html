More about this

Uses upstream's own battle engine and its default options (shields averaged over 0 and 1, shield baiting on, meta threats prioritized, shadows included). Each Pokémon's level is worked out from its species, IVs and CP. After grading, this page's address holds the team, so it can be bookmarked or shared.
