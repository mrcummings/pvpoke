Team Finder

Build a pool of contenders, then search every team of three from it for the grades you want. Grades follow pvpoke.com's Team Builder, using upstream's own battle engine and its default options (shields averaged over 0 and 1, shield baiting on, meta threats prioritized, shadows included). Each Pokémon's level is worked out from its species, IVs and CP.

Contenders

Each cup keeps its own list, remembered in this browser. Export a CSV to keep a copy. A CSV needs the columns speciesId, ivAtk, ivDef, ivHp, cp, fastMove, chargedMove1, chargedMove2 (any order, other columns ignored); species and moves can be IDs or names, a blank move gets the recommended one, and none leaves the second charged move empty. Loaded and added contenders go on the end of the list; repeats are skipped.

    Battles

    Each contender battles every Pokémon eligible in this cup once (0 and 1 shields, as the Team Builder does). The search then grades every team of three from these results. Results last until the page is closed or reloaded; contenders already done are skipped.

    Find teams

    Grades every team of three from the contenders (their "In search" setting decides which), the same way as "Grade one team", and lists the best that meet your targets. Contenders that haven't battled yet battle first.
