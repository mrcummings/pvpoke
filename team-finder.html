Team Finder

{% include team-finder-nav.html %}

Build a pool of contenders, then search every team of three from it for the grades you want.

More about this

Grades follow pvpoke.com's Team Builder, using upstream's own battle engine and its default options (shields averaged over 0 and 1, shield baiting on, meta threats prioritized, shadows included). Each Pokémon's level is worked out from its species, IVs and CP.

{% include team-finder-cup.html %}

Each cup keeps its own list, remembered in this browser. Export a CSV to keep a copy.

About the CSV

A CSV needs the columns speciesId, ivAtk, ivDef, ivHp, cp, fastMove, chargedMove1, chargedMove2 (any order, other columns ignored); species and moves can be IDs or names, a blank move gets the recommended one, and none leaves the second charged move empty. Loaded and added contenders go on the end of the list; repeats are skipped. An exported CSV loads back in: its extra columns are ignored. Upgraded versions (the Upgrade… button) aren't exported.

    Battles

    Each contender battles every Pokémon eligible in this cup once, which fills in its Wins.

    More about this

    Battles use 0 and 1 shields, as the Team Builder does. Find teams grades every team of three from these results, and battles any contender that hasn't been done yet itself. Results last until the page is closed or reloaded; contenders already done are skipped.

    Grades every team of three from the contenders and lists the best that meet your targets.

    More about this

    Each contender's "In search" setting (on the Contenders tab) decides whether it's used, must be in every team, or is left out. Teams are graded the same way as on the Grade one team page; contenders that haven't battled yet battle first. If no team meets the targets, the closest teams are shown instead.
